NOTICE REGARDING SUBMISSION OF PROPERTY TAX AND WATER CHARGES

Notice is hereby given to all residents of the village that the annual property tax and water charges for the financial year 2026-27 are due for payment. In pursuance of the Panchayat resolution dated 02/03/2026, every owner or occupier of a house shall pay the dues on or before 30/06/2026 at the Gram Panchayat office.

Residents who pay the full amount on or before 31/05/2026 will be eligible for a rebate of 5 percent on the total dues. Defaulters who fail to pay by 30/06/2026 shall be liable to pay a penalty of Rs. 50 per month on the unpaid amount, in addition to the dues.

The following documents must be produced at the time of payment:
1. Previous year's tax receipt
2. Aadhaar card or any photo identity proof
3. Property assessment number

Residents who wish to dispute the assessed amount may submit a written objection to the Gram Sevak within 15 days of receiving this notice. Objections received after this period shall not be entertained.

For further details, contact the Gram Sevak during office hours, 10:00 am to 5:00 pm, Monday to Saturday.
