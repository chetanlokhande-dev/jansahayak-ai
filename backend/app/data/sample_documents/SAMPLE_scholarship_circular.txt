Circular No. SAMPLE/EDU/2026/58
Date: 05/08/2026

SUBJECT: Invitation of applications for the Sample Merit Scholarship 2026-27

1. Applications are invited from students of Class 11 and 12 studying in recognised schools of the state for the Sample Merit Scholarship.

2. Eligibility: The applicant must have secured at least 75 percent marks in the previous annual examination. The annual family income of the applicant shall not exceed Rs. 3,00,000. The applicant must be a resident of the state.

3. Benefit: Selected students will receive a scholarship of Rs. 10,000 per year, which will be credited directly to the student's bank account.

4. Documents required: (a) Mark sheet of the previous examination, (b) Income certificate issued by the competent authority, (c) Residence certificate, (d) Bank passbook copy, (e) Aadhaar card.

5. How to apply: Applications must be submitted online through the department's sample portal. The last date for submission of applications is 15/09/2026. Applications received after the last date will not be considered.

6. The school principal shall verify the applications and forward them to the District Education Officer by 30/09/2026.
